# 08 Compliance and Legal

This is not legal advice. Hire a lawyer who works with adult businesses. The items below are what a professional setup normally covers, and processors will ask for them.

## Age gating
1. Visitor age confirmation screen before any content.
2. Many regions now require real age verification (ID or face estimation) for adult sites. Plan to plug in a verification provider and make it switchable by country.
3. Store only the result and timestamp, not the ID image, where possible.

## Performer verification and records
The creator and anyone appearing in content must be verified adults who consented.
1. Collect and securely store government ID and a signed consent and release form for every performer in every video.
2. In the US, record keeping rules (often called 2257) require keeping these records and a custodian of records address displayed on the site. Other countries have their own rules. Nigeria and other regions have their own laws on adult content distribution, so the creator must understand where he and the host are based and where he sells.
3. Use the compliance_records table and encrypted storage. Only the owner can view.

## Zero tolerance content
Never allow content involving minors, non consenting people, or anything illegal. No user uploads in v1, which keeps this simple. If you ever add fan uploads or comments, add moderation.

## Legal pages needed
1. Terms of service
2. Privacy policy (what you collect, retention, deletion on request)
3. Refund and chargeback policy
4. DMCA and takedown policy with a contact
5. Cookie notice where required
6. Record keeping statement
7. Content removal request form (for anyone depicted)

## Business setup
* Register a business entity for the creator so payouts and contracts are clean.
* Separate business bank account.
* Get written approval from hosting, CDN, email, and processor.

## Privacy
* Hash IPs, store minimum data, allow account deletion.
* Customers are sensitive about discretion. Never expose buyer lists. Encrypt email at rest if possible.
* Neutral email subjects and sender name.

## Takedown handling
Log requests, respond within 48 hours, remove disputed content first and investigate after.

## Advertising limits
Most ad networks and social platforms restrict adult promotion. Use organic channels, Telegram, and compliant adult ad networks. Keep preview images tame on public social posts.
