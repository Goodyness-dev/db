# 10 Growth and Funnel

## Core idea
The creator already has an audience on Telegram (his public channel has over 11 thousand views per post). Keep Telegram as the top of the funnel and move the money to the site.

## Funnel
1. Public Telegram channel posts teasers with a link to the site (use a short branded link).
2. Link opens the video page, one tap to checkout.
3. After purchase, fan gets a prompt to join a private Telegram group or membership.
4. Subscribers get early drops and subscriber only posts.
5. Telegram bot announces new drops and sends receipts or access links.

## Telegram bot ideas
* /latest shows newest drops with buy links
* /status shows subscription status and renewal date
* Alerts to the creator on sales
* Auto invite paid subscribers to the private group and remove them when expired (use the bot with invite links and expiry)

## Conversion boosters
* Guest checkout, minimal fields
* Show the membership value: "12 videos this month for the price of 2"
* Countdown on limited bundles
* Social proof counters (total members), only if accurate
* Abandoned checkout email after 1 hour
* Win back emails to expired subscribers

## Retention
* Weekly drop schedule so fans return on a set day
* Subscriber only polls to decide next content
* Loyalty perks: free video after 6 months
* Birthday or anniversary discount

## SEO
* Server rendered pages with unique titles and descriptions
* Sitemap and structured data (keep it compliant)
* Blog or news posts about drops
* Note: adult SEO is competitive, social and Telegram will do most of the work early

## Analytics to watch
Visit to checkout rate, checkout to paid rate, average order value, subscriber churn, lifetime value, traffic by source.

## Experiments
Test prices (for example 10 versus 15), teaser lengths, headline copy, and button colors. Run one test at a time.

## Email list
Collect emails at checkout. Email is the one channel nobody can ban the creator from, so build it from day one.
