# 01 Concept and Brand

## Positioning
A private members club feel, not a cheap tube site. Think luxury streetwear drop meets streaming platform. Black, minimal, confident, fast.

## Brand pillars
1. Exclusive: scarcity language, drops, limited time bundles.
2. Effortless: browse, pay, watch in under 60 seconds.
3. Discreet: neutral billing descriptor, private by default, no public buyer lists.
4. Premium: cinematic previews, sharp type, smooth motion.

## Visitor journey
1. Lands on the age gate (18 plus confirmation).
2. Sees the home page: hero reel, latest drops, subscription pitch.
3. Browses the catalog, hovers cards to see blurred or short teaser loops.
4. Clicks a video: detail page with teaser, price, buy button.
5. Checks out as guest (email only) or logs in.
6. Content unlocks instantly, also emailed as a secure link.
7. Gets nudged to subscribe for everything.

## Pages
### Public
* Age gate modal
* Home: hero, trending, new drops, subscribe banner, FAQ
* Catalog: grid with filters (category, price, newest, popular), search
* Video detail: teaser player, description, price, buy or subscribe buttons, related items
* Pricing: free tier (teasers), pay per video, monthly membership
* About the creator
* Contact and support
* Legal pages: terms, privacy, 2257 style compliance statement, DMCA, refund policy

### Member
* Library: purchased and subscription content
* Watch page: protected player
* Billing: subscription status, next renewal date, cancel, update card, receipts
* Account: email, password, delete account

### Admin (see 06_admin_dashboard.md)

## Content types
1. Single video (pay per view)
2. Photo set
3. Bundle (several items at a discount)
4. Subscription only content
5. Custom request (optional later, manual workflow)
6. Livestream replay (optional later)

## Monetization levers
* Pay per video
* Monthly membership (the recurring money)
* Bundles and seasonal sales
* Tips (a simple amount field at checkout)
* Promo codes and referral codes
* Early access drops for subscribers

## Copy tone
Short lines. Direct. Confident. Example: "New drop. 4K. Gone from the front page in 7 days." Avoid walls of text.
